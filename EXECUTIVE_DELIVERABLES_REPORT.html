3. Critical Calculation & Accounting Glitches Solved (Today)

During the MD-side and test team retest, 4 high-severity financial and payroll calculation bugs were identified and eliminated:

Defect Title Root Cause Discovered Engineering Resolution & Integrity Guard
1. Receipt Disappearing Glitch
₹100 receipt in history showed ₹0 on project & dashboard		 Receipts created in Accounts were saved with status: 'confirmed'. However, Project Detail and Dashboard summary queries strictly filtered for status === 'received' or 'completed', discarding confirmed receipts. Updated financial query filters across Project Detail and Dashboard to accept 'confirmed' receipts alongside 'received' and 'completed'. Receipt totals now immediately reflect in all project cards.
2. Dashboard vs P&L Contract Mismatch
Contract totals differed by ₹500		 The Dashboard quotation query did not filter by approved status and was restricted to MD, while Reports P&L included approved variations (QTN-2 for ₹500 on QA Project). Standardized contract valuation formula across Dashboard, Projects Index, Project Detail, and P&L Statements to strictly use Base + Approved Quotations. Synchronized data access for both MD and Accounts.
3. Monthly Office Salary Over-Multiplication
₹30,000 monthly salary became ₹45,000 for 1 Sunday shift		 Attendance save and calculation handlers multiplied day_value * employee.wage_rate directly. For a monthly employee (₹30,000/mo), a 12-hour Sunday shift (1.5 days) produced 1.5 × ₹30,000 = ₹45,000. Introduced getDailyWageRate() in wageRules.ts pro-rating monthly salaries on standard 30-day basis (₹1,000/day). A 1.5-day Sunday shift now accurately records ₹1,500. Sanitized corrupted database entries.
4. Historical Attendance Cost Mutation
Changing wage from ₹600 to ₹700 altered past attendance sheets		 Weekly and monthly attendance grids calculated worker wages on render using totalDays * emp.wage_rate (the current master rate), retroactively recalculating past audited weeks when rate changed. Attendance calculations now sum each record's historically locked rec.calculated_wage and rec.wage_rate. Editing employee wage rates in Masters now only applies to future days, protecting past payroll.

Created dedicated unit tests in src/tests/duplicateAndReceiptFixes.test.ts and src/tests/wageRules.test.ts to permanently guard these 4 calculations against any future regressions.
